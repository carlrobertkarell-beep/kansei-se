Kansei Rehabcenter is a musculoskeletal clinic in central Stockholm combining manual therapy (naprapathy), diagnostic ultrasound and ultrasound-guided injections, so you get answers and treatment without weeks of waiting. No referral needed, and yes: we are happy to see you in English.

What we treat

Back and neck pain, sciatica, shoulder problems (impingement, frozen shoulder, calcific tendinitis), tennis elbow, runner's and jumper's knee, knee and hip osteoarthritis, Achilles problems, plantar fasciitis, and most other joint, muscle, tendon and nerve-related complaints.

Why patients choose us

  • Same-visit answers. Clinical examination and diagnostic ultrasound under one roof, no waiting weeks for imaging.
  • Assessment before any needle. We never sell injections; we treat diagnoses, and we will tell you honestly when an injection is not the right path.
  • A written plan, always. You leave knowing what is wrong, what we will do about it, and what you can do yourself.

Good to know

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly, most patients come to us without any referral.

Is the online booking in English?

The booking platform (Bokadirekt) is primarily in Swedish. If you prefer, simply call or email info@kansei.se and we will find you a time in English.

Does private health insurance cover the visit?

Many private and employer-provided insurance plans cover naprapathy and our other services, check with your provider, and we will supply receipts and documentation.

Where are you located?

Upplandsgatan 26, 113 60 Stockholm, a two-minute walk from Odenplan station (metro and commuter rail).

How quickly can I get an appointment?

We keep generous opening hours, weekdays 08–19 and weekends 10–15, so most patients find a time within days. Check live availability via the booking link.
